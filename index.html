How the first one runs

About 90 minutes. Bring a laptop if you want to show something. Not required.

01

Introductions

Who you are, what you do, and how much you have played with this stuff. "Barely" is a fine answer.

02

Show and tell

One person walks through something they are working on. Real project, real screen, real rough edges. Questions welcome throughout.

03

The go-around

Everyone takes a turn and shares three things:

01 builtOne thing you have built or used AI for
02 learnedOne thing you learned, even if it was "that didn't work"
03 wantOne thing you hope to take away from this group
04

Open discussion

Dig into whatever the go-around surfaced. Compare tools, swap prompts, pair up on a problem. Then we pick a date and a presenter for next time.
